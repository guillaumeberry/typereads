Gerrit Noordzij's concise theory of writing, in which all letters, type included, descend from handwriting, and typography is “writing with prefabricated characters”. The theory is built on the stroke and its two models, translation and expansion. First published in Dutch as De streek (1985); translated into English by Peter Enneson.
