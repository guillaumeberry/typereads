A print journal on typography and graphic design from the London foundry Fontsmith, grown out of its TypeNotes blog. Designed by The Counter Press and edited by Emily Gosling, it mixes essays, interviews and photo essays, on subjects from type in film to optical illusions. Each issue was limited to 3,000 copies.
