A practical study of archival research and revival design by Céline Hurka and Nóra Békés, graduates of the Royal Academy of Art in The Hague. Hurka revisits the Renaissance types of Claude Garamont and Robert Granjon, Békés the Baroque type of Miklós Kis Misztótfalusi. 160 pages, with a foreword by Frank E. Blokland.
