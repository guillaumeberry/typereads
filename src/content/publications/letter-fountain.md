A 640-page reference on the anatomy of type: the form of every letter, punctuation mark and special character, specimens of 35 classic typefaces, and links between type design and historical movements. Begun in 1994 as a reference for students at the St Joost Academy in Breda, it comes with three ribbon bookmarks and a typographic ruler.
