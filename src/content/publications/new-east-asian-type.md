Chinese, Japanese and Korean writing systems share thousands of characters, yet no single font can serve them all. Through essays by type designers and researchers — illustrated with historical material, type specimens and practical design suggestions — this book examines the historical, linguistic and deeply cultural reasons why this is the case. This is a book for type designers, graphic designers working with multilingual typesetting, and researchers interested in the cultural and historical forces that shape the writing systems of the world.
