A 656-page book showing all 109,242 characters encoded in Unicode at the time, with a 60-page introduction to Unicode in German. It grew out of decodeunicode.org, Johannes Bergerhausen's online database of the world's writing systems at Hochschule Mainz. It won the gold Design Award of the Federal Republic of Germany.
